Project Proposal

Find Car

Using an LLM to understand what people write when they look for a car

Omer Maimon & Gal Amano
Building Conversational AI Systems with Large Language Models and Agents
Hi everyone, we are Omer and Gal. Our project is called Find Car. It is about using an LLM to understand what people write when they look for a car. This is a first idea. It is not a finished system.
The problem

People describe cars in free text

A buyer writes
אני רוצה סוזוקי אדומה, משנת 2000 עד 2010
A seller writes
למכירה סוזוקי אדומה, שנת 2006, מחיר 25,000 ₪. רכב שמור, יד ראשונה
Two free texts. No common structure. Hard to compare.
Find Car
Buyers write what they want in their own words. Sellers write their ads in their own words too. Both are free text, and they look very different. This makes it hard to match a buyer to a car automatically. These are only examples we wrote to show the idea.

This is the whole idea in one picture. On the customer side, an LLM reads the request and fills a profile. On the seller side, an LLM reads the ad and finds the car details. Then we match the two sides to find relevant cars. How we get the ads is only background. It is not the focus of our project.

Find Car
Now the customer answered the budget question. Most details go into fixed fields. But the customer also said something that has no field: they need a car for driving with kids. We keep it in the notes block, so we do not lose it. The system must not invent notes that the customer did not say. If something is not mentioned, it stays as not stated.

Ad B does not fit: the color and the year are wrong. This is a simple example. We have not decided on any scoring method yet.

Find Car
This last part is a plan. It is not done yet. We want to prepare a set of example texts ourselves. Next to each text we write the correct output: which detail goes to which field, what goes to the notes, and what was not said. There are two ways to use examples. One: put a few examples in the instructions. The model stays the same. Two: train the model on many examples, so the model itself learns. We plan to compare both. We have not chosen a model or a method yet. Thank you!
